## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the serene, fertile, and welcoming spirit of a highland rural village. The brand persona is that of a warm community host: sincere, unhurried, grounded in agricultural heritage, and in harmony with pristine mountain nature.

The visual style blends **Contemporary Organic Warmth** with **Humanist Minimalism**:
- Spacious, sunlight-filled layouts evoking open terraced fields and fresh mountain air.
- Warm, earthy tactile qualities avoiding sterile tech aesthetics; prioritizing approachable comfort.
- Clear, welcoming visual hierarchies designed primarily for travelers, families, and eco-tourists browsing on mobile devices.
- High-contrast, friendly focal points directing visitors naturally toward homestay bookings, guided farm treks, and community contact via messaging.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the natural panorama of a lush mountain slope:

- **Primary (`#1B4D3E` — Forest Pine / Deep Emerald):** Represents highland foliage and misty canopy forests. Used for top-level headers, primary brand anchors, key navigation states, and grounded structural elements.
- **Secondary (`#C26D38` — Warm Terracotta / Clay Timber):** Echoes red volcanic soil, handwoven bamboo crafts, and traditional village woodwork. Deployed for secondary badges, tour package price highlights, curated tags, and warm interactive accents.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366` — Fresh WhatsApp Green / Vitality Sprout):** A vivid, instantly recognizable action green dedicated strictly to high-conversion inquiries, WhatsApp booking triggers, and live status beacons.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7EE` — Soft Cream Sand / Bleached Cotton):** The core canvas background, eliminating harsh clinical white in favor of the comforting temperature of morning sunlight on stone.
- **Supporting Accents:**
  - **Sage Mist (`#87A987`):** For category chips, soothing card backgrounds, and organic divider tints.
  - **Earthy Deep Charcoal (`#222B25`):** High-legibility text tone carrying subtle pine undertones for long-form reading comfort.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** serves as the universal family across all typographic roles. Its open apertures, friendly rounded geometric skeleton, and crisp Indonesian roots provide effortless readability for tourists of all age groups, while rendering reliably on low-bandwidth mobile devices.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set with strong weights (`700` and `800`) and slight negative letter-spacing to build solid, welcoming titles reminiscent of botanical signposts and community lodge signage.
- **Body:** Kept open with relaxed line-heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) to facilitate fatigue-free reading of cultural histories, itinerary details, and travel guidelines.
- **Labels & Badges:** Slightly tracked with bold weights for swift glancing of tour package tags, homestay amenities, and availability badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive, fluid grid system designed with a **mobile-first** travel discovery journey in mind:

- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack with `1rem` side margins. Tour cards, village facilities, and gallery previews leverage horizontal snap-carousels to conserve vertical space while preserving immersive imagery.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid, `2rem` screen margins, and `1rem` column gutters. Packages and activities reflow into 2-column card pairs.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a comfortable maximum container width of `1200px`, buffered by `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters to preserve the calm, unhurried visual pace.

Vertical spacing follows generous multiples of `space-md` (`1rem`) and `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) to let scenic photography breathe and maintain a soothing, scenic rhythm between community sections.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an authentic, earthy feel, visual depth avoids artificial drop shadows and glossy synthetic layers:

- **Ambient Sun-Cast Shadows:** Soft, highly diffused shadows warm-tinted with raw umber rather than cold black (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(54, 45, 30, 0.08)`). Used sparingly on hover states for tour cards and sticky contact controls.
- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is primarily established through stacked earthy surfaces. Cards sit on warm cloud white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale sage cream surfaces over the neutral canvas (`#FAF7EE`).
- **Organic Borders:** Thin, soft borders in 10% opacity forest green (`rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.12)`) establish clear definition between itinerary modules without breaking visual softness.

## Shapes

The shape scale is set to `2` (Rounded). Standard elements employ `0.5rem` (8px) border radiuses, large interactive surfaces and cards use `1rem` (16px), and prominent feature hero containers or image frames use `1.5rem` (24px).

Buttons and category chips adopt pill-like curvatures (`9999px`) to emphasize warmth, touchability, and natural pebble-like aesthetics. Images of terraced fields, local artisans, and natural streams feature rounded container corners to mirror organic landscape silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **WhatsApp Direct Action Button (Tertiary):** Built with `#25D366` background, crisp white label, and a WhatsApp chat glyph. Features an elevated pill shape with a warm, subtle hover bloom (`0 6px 20px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)`). Fixed to the bottom right on mobile screens for effortless one-thumb booking inquiries.
- **Primary Button:** Background `#1B4D3E` with `#FFFFFF` text. Used for main site interactions such as "Jelajahi Paket Wisata" (Explore Tour Packages) and "Unduh Peta Desa" (Download Village Map).
- **Secondary Button:** Warm Terracotta border (`#C26D38`) with transparent interior and terracotta text, shifting to a soft terracotta wash on hover.

### Tourism Package Cards
- Composed with an aspect-ratio-bound image at the top (16:10), followed by an inner padded container (`1.25rem`).
- Features category badge pill overlays (e.g., "Edukasi Tani", "Trekking Curug"), dynamic duration badges, and high-visibility price tags rendered in bold Terracotta (`#C26D38`).
- Concludes with an explicit single-tap "Detail Paket & Reservasi" action.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Compact pill-shaped containers with a `0.25rem` border radius or full pill style.
- Unselected: Soft Sage Cream background (`#E8EFE9`) with muted Forest Green text.
- Selected: Deep Forest Green background (`#1B4D3E`) with crisp white text.

### Village Photo Gallery
- Masonry or flexible grid featuring rounded-lg photo frames (`1rem` radius).
- Gentle hover zoom transition (`transform: scale(1.02)`) with a warm gradient scrim (`rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.4)`) revealing short contextual captions like "Panen Kopi Pagi Hari" or "Sunrise Bukit Asri".

### Inputs & Form Controls
- Soft sand-tinted inputs (`#FFFFFF` with `#D8D2C2` outline) that glow with a forest pine border ring (`#1B4D3E`) on focus.
- Generous touch targets (minimum height `48px`) suited for outdoor, on-the-go mobile entry.

### Community Notice & Weather Alert Banners
- Low-saturation sage and amber alerts informing visitors of current mountain weather, guided walk schedules, and local harvest festival timings.
